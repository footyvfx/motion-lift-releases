How it works

Track the move

With After Effects' own tracker, Mocha AE or Motion Sketch, so the property has a key on every frame.

Click Lift

Motion Lift finds where each move starts and stops and fits Bezier eases to the reference's speed. One undo step.

Edit like your own keys

You get a few ordinary keyframes. The shake goes on an "ML Shake" effect, switched off, to keep or delete.

Everything in the panel

One panel docked in After Effects. Lift is the heart of it; the rest takes the slow jobs around it.

Lift

Install

1. Download

Get MotionLift-x.y.z.zxp from the latest release (Pro buyers also get it with their receipt).

2. Install the .zxp

With a free ZXP installer such as aescripts' ZXP Installer or Anastasiy's Extension Manager. Restart After Effects, then Window > Extensions > Motion Lift.

3. Pro: enter your key

Open Licence at the bottom of the panel, paste the key from your receipt email, click Activate.

Updates

The panel checks for a new version once a day and installs it when you click Install. Restart After Effects to finish. On Windows it opens the download instead (Windows keeps the panel's files in use): install it with your ZXP installer.

Licence, privacy and refunds

Your licence

  • One Pro licence is for one computer.
  • To move it, click Deactivate in the panel on the old computer, then activate on the new one.
  • After activating, Pro works offline for up to 14 days at a time; it renews itself when you're online.
  • Please don't share your key or the installer files. Anyone can download Lite from this page.

What the panel sends

  • When you activate or renew Pro: your licence key, a one-way hash of your computer's hardware id (not the id itself) and the Motion Lift version, to our licence server, which checks the key with Lemon Squeezy.
  • Once a day: a request for the latest version number, from GitHub.
  • Nothing else. Your footage, projects and keyframes never leave your computer, and there's no tracking or analytics.

Refunds

All sales are final. If your key won't activate or Pro doesn't work on your computer, email us and we'll sort it out.

Requirements

After Effects 2022 (22.0) or later, on a Mac or a Windows PC. Windows support is new: if anything doesn't work on your PC, email us and we'll sort it out. Track for me also needs the free tool uv (astral.sh/uv; on Windows, its PowerShell line); the panel tells you how to get it.
