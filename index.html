Install. Up and running in a minute.

Full guide ›
  1. Download

    Get the .zxp free from GitHub. Pro buyers also get it with their receipt.

  2. Get a free installer

    After Effects can’t open a .zxp by itself. Use aescripts ZXP/UXP Installer or Anastasiy’s Extension Manager.

  3. Install

    Quit After Effects, open the installer and drag the .zxp onto its window.

  4. Open it

    In After Effects: Window > Extensions > Motion Lift. Pro: paste your key under Licence, then Activate.
